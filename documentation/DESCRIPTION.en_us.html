Squarol is a heavy, wide, rounded-square display typeface made for logos, wordmarks, headlines and packaging. Its signature is the "leaf corner": convex corners on the top-left to bottom-right diagonal are softened, while the opposite pair stays sharp, giving every word a sense of forward motion.

The family currently ships one style and covers the Google Fonts Latin Core character set. To contribute, see github.com/TOMOOLY/squarol.
